Kids & School

Enrollment, daily contact, communicating with teachers — handling everything for your child.

Japan's School System:

  • Nursery (保育園): ages 0-5, for working parents
  • Kindergarten (幼稚園): ages 3-5, education-focused
  • Elementary school (小学校): ages 6-12, 6 years
  • Junior high (中学校): ages 12-15, 3 years (compulsory)
  • High school (高校): ages 15-18, 3 years

Enrollment Procedures:

  • Public elementary/junior high: register at city hall, school assignment is automatic
  • Nursery/kindergarten: apply at city hall or directly to the school
  • Required documents: residence card, resident register (住民票), and vaccination records
  • Japanese language support varies by school — ask in advance

Daily Communication with School:

  • Absence: call the school before morning or use the school app if available
  • Late arrival/early pickup: notify the school in advance
  • Notices sent home (プリント): read carefully, sign and return when required
  • Teacher contact: through the school office, or at drop-off/pickup time

School Lunch & Costs:

  • School lunch (給食): served at most public elementary and junior high schools
  • Monthly lunch fee: around ¥4,000-5,000
  • Allergy concerns must be reported to the school in advance
  • Some nurseries/kindergartens require packed lunches on certain days

Fees & Free Education Policy:

  • Public elementary and junior high: tuition free (compulsory education)
  • Nursery/kindergarten (幼児教育無償化): children aged 3-5 are eligible for free education
  • Some fees still apply: school supplies, excursions, extracurricular activities
  • Income-based subsidies available — inquire at city hall

实用句子 / Phrases

Enrollment & Transfer

ことし、こどもがしょうがっこうににゅうがくします。てつづきはどうすればいいですか。

▶ My child is starting elementary school this year. What do I need to do?

にゅうえんのもうしこみをしたいです。

▶ I'd like to apply for enrollment at the nursery.

ひっこしてきたので、てんこうのてつづきをおねがいします。

▶ We've moved, so I'd like to arrange a school transfer.

ひつようなしょるいをおしえてください。

▶ Could you tell me what documents are required?

がいこくせきでもにゅうがくできますか。

▶ Can foreign nationals enroll?

こどもはにほんごがほとんどはなせません。にほんごのサポートはありますか。

▶ My child barely speaks Japanese. Is there any Japanese language support?

しょくもつアレルギーがあるので、きゅうしょくについてそうだんしたいです。

▶ My child has food allergies. I'd like to discuss the school lunch.

にゅうがくとどけとじゅうみんひょうをごていしゅつください。

◀ Please submit the enrollment form and resident registration.

ぼこくのがっこうのせいせきしょうめいしょはひつようですか。

▶ Is a school record from my home country required?

Daily Drop-off & Pick-up

おはようございます。よろしくおねがいします。

▶ Good morning. Please take care of them today.

さくやからすこしねつがあります。

▶ They've had a slight fever since last night.

すこしせきがあります。ねつはありません。

▶ They have a slight cough, but no fever.

きょうははやめにむかえにきます。

▶ I'll come to pick them up a bit early today.

おむかえがすこしおくれます。

▶ I'll be a little late picking them up.

きょうはそふぼがむかえにきます。

▶ Their grandparents will come to pick them up today.

おこさんのぐあいがわるそうです。むかえにきていただけますか。

◀ Your child doesn't seem to be feeling well. Could you come and pick them up?

きょうのプリントです。サインをおねがいします。

◀ Here are today's notices. Please sign here.

Absences & Contact

こどもがねつをだしたので、ほんじつはおやすみします。

▶ My child has a fever, so they'll be absent today.

びょういんによってからとうこうするため、すこしおくれます。

▶ We're going to the doctor first, so they'll be a little late.

ほんじつはようじがあるため、そうたいさせたいんですが。

▶ We have an errand today, so I'd like to pick them up early.

かんせんしょうのため、しゅっせきていしになりました。

▶ They've been placed on attendance suspension due to an infectious illness.

あしたからとうえんできます。

▶ They can return starting tomorrow.

きのう、ふでばこをがっこうにわすれてしまったようです。

▶ It seems my child left their pencil case at school yesterday.

うちのこはにほんごがまだふじゅうぶんなので、よろしくおねがいします。

▶ My child's Japanese is still not fluent. Thank you for your patience.

おだいじになさってください。

◀ Please take care.

Illness & Injury

おこさんがはつねつしています。むかえにきていただけますか。

◀ Your child has a fever. Could you come and pick them up?

いますぐむかえにいきます。

▶ I'm on my way right now.

がっこうでケガをしたときいたのですが、いまのじょうきょうをおしえてください。

▶ I heard my child was injured at school. Could you tell me what happened?

ほけんしつでてあてをしました。げんざいはおちついています。

◀ We treated them in the nurse's office. They've calmed down now.

びょういんにつれていくひつようはありますか。

▶ Do I need to take them to the hospital?

じこのじょうきょうをくわしくおしえていただけますか。

▶ Could you tell me in detail how the accident happened?

Talking with Teachers

うちのこのがっこうでのようすをきかせていただけますか。

▶ Could you tell me how my child is getting on at school?

さいきん、べんきょうについていけないようですが、どうすればいいでしょうか。

▶ My child seems to be struggling to keep up with their studies. What should I do?

ともだちとうまくやれているかしんぱいです。

▶ I'm worried about whether my child is getting along with their friends.

いちど、こじんめんだんをおねがいできますか。

▶ Could we arrange a one-on-one meeting?

おこさんはじゅぎょうちゅう、よくてをあげてはっぴょうしていますよ。

◀ Your child often raises their hand to answer in class.

なにかしんぱいなことがありましたら、いつでもごれんらくください。

◀ If you have any concerns, please feel free to contact me anytime.

ごかていでもようすをみてあげてください。

◀ Please also keep an eye on them at home.

▶ Could you explain what this letter says?

きょうのしゅくだいのはんいをおしえていただけますか。

▶ Could you tell me what homework was assigned today?

らいしゅうのえんそくにもっていくものをおしえてください。

▶ Could you tell me what to bring for next week's field trip?
