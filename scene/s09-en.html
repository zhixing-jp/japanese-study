Housing

Finding a place, repairs, receiving packages — solving housing-related problems.

Renting an Apartment in Japan:

  • Search → View property → Apply → Screening (審査) → Sign contract → Pay initial fees → Move in
  • Screening for foreign nationals may require a guarantor company (保証会社)
  • Typical contract term: 2 years (auto-renewal with renewal fee)

Initial Costs (One-time Payment on Move-in):

  • Security deposit (敷金): 1-2 months' rent, returned on move-out (minus deductions)
  • Key money (礼金): 0-2 months' rent, not returned (custom gift to landlord)
  • Agency fee (仲介手数料): usually 1 month's rent
  • Fire insurance (火災保険): required, about ¥15,000-20,000 per 2 years
  • Guarantor company fee (保証会社): about 0.5-1 month's rent

Things to Note When Renting:

  • Read the contract carefully — especially restoration to original condition (原状回復) clauses
  • Pets, musical instruments, and subletting usually require special permission
  • Report repairs to the landlord or management company promptly
  • Garbage rules vary by area — confirm with your landlord

What to Do After Moving In:

  • Register your new address at city hall within 14 days
  • Update your residence card address
  • Set up electricity, gas, and water contracts
  • Notify your bank, workplace, and insurance of your new address

实用句子 / Phrases

Finding a Place

がいこくじんでもかりられるぶっけんをさがしています。

▶ I'm looking for a place that accepts foreign nationals.

やちんはかんりひこみではちまんえんいないでおねがいします。

▶ My budget, including management fees, is under 80,000 yen.

えきまであるいてじゅっぷんいないのところがきぼうです。

▶ I'd prefer within 10 minutes' walk from the station.

このへやをみせてもらえますか。

▶ Could I take a look at this room?

せんたくきおきばはしつないにありますか。

▶ Is the washing machine space indoors?

インターネットのかいせんはすでにひかれていますか。

▶ Is the internet line already set up?

ペットはかえますか。

▶ Are pets allowed?

ちゅうしゃじょうはありますか。

▶ Is there a parking space?

ちくねんすうはどのくらいですか。

▶ How old is the building?

しょきひよう（しききん・れいきんなど）のみつもりをおつくりします。

◀ I'll prepare a breakdown of the initial costs (deposit, key money, etc.).

こちらのぶっけんは、がいこくせきのかたはほしょうがいしゃのしんさがひつようです。

◀ For this property, foreign nationals will need to pass a guarantor company review.
